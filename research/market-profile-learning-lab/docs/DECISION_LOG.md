# Decision Log

## D001 — Central analysis, not per-browser analysis

The backend analyzes an eligible shift once and stores the result. Replay and
live browser tabs retrieve the same versioned commentary.

## D002 — Shift-triggered analysis

CE, PE, Futures-OI, and BankNifty-reference Futures-volume control migrations
are the event source. Same-receipt migrations are consolidated before analysis.

## D003 — Compact commentary first

The GUI leads with: what shifted, what it means, the fixed-horizon possible
outcome, support/resistance candidates, confidence, and invalidation. Full
evidence remains expandable.

## D004 — Explanation and prediction are separately testable

Descriptive accuracy is not counted as directional accuracy. Direction,
calibration, abstention, and level behavior receive independent metrics.

## D005 — Codex proposes agents; deterministic code scores them

The lab does not change model weights. Codex receives training-only aggregate
statistics and proposes constrained, versioned specifications. Forecasts and
scores are generated by deterministic code.

## D006 — Strict causal prefix

Future observations, transitions, OI, volume, inventory, and labels are
excluded from candidate inputs. Labels are stored separately and bound to the
complete case hash.

## D007 — Support/resistance requires a reaction test

Being close to a future high or low is insufficient. The next evaluator must
distinguish a touch followed by a configured reaction from a touch followed by
a configured breach, in the correct time order.

## D008 — The August pilot did not pass

The best learned candidate did not beat the best deterministic baseline on the
aggregate validation gate. The decision is
`NO_CANDIDATE_EDGE_ON_VALIDATION`; holdout remains sealed.

## D009 — Horizon specialists may be evaluated

The aggregate gate averages 5, 15, and 30 minutes. The next evaluation may
predeclare separate horizon specialists, but it must account for session-level
stability before the holdout is opened.

## D010 — BankNifty and NIFTY share contracts, not fitted weights

The causal engine, schemas, scoring, and commentary lifecycle are reusable.
Instrument symbols, expiry resolution, strike geometry, thresholds, data
availability, and trained candidate parameters remain instrument-specific.

## D011 — GitHub contains reproducible source, not market data

Commit source, schemas, tests, methodology, small aggregate evidence, and
manifests. Exclude raw records, browser payloads, run workspaces, logs,
credentials, tokens, environments, and archive binaries.

## D012 — Existing production and frozen research remain untouched

This research addition does not alter the live framework, collectors, replay,
services, ports, firewall rules, verified tags, or sealed pilot artifacts.
